Opening Ternline on macOS

This release is unsigned and not notarized by Apple. If you trust this
download, follow these steps to approve Ternline on its first launch:

1. Drag Ternline.app into Applications.
2. Open Ternline from Applications. If macOS says the developer cannot be
   verified or Apple cannot check the app, dismiss the warning.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down.
4. Click Open Anyway for Ternline, then confirm Open. macOS may ask for
   your password or Touch ID.

Try opening Ternline before looking for Open Anyway. After approval, you
can open it normally from Applications.

Apple's instructions:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
